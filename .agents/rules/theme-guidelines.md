# Power Diagnostics Design Rule: Theme Blue Enforcement

1. **Theme Blue Palette**:
   - Primary: `#2563eb`
   - Dark: `#1d4ed8`
   - Gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #2563eb 0%, #1d4ed8 100%)`

2. **All Hero Header Banners & CTA Buttons**:
   - Must use Theme Blue (`#2563eb` / `#1d4ed8` gradient) with crisp white text (`#ffffff`).
   - Do NOT use dark slate (`#0f172a`) or black for hero banners.

3. **Exceptions**:
   - Only WhatsApp (`#25D366`) and social media buttons retain their official brand colors.
